Privacy Policy

Learn how we process and protect your personal data.

This Privacy Policy explains the rules for processing personal data submitted through the contact form available on the Ardium website.

1. Data controller

The controller of your personal data is Ardium. For matters concerning your personal data, contact us at contact@ardium.pl.

2. What data we process

When you use the contact form, we process the data you provide: your name, email address, message subject and message content. We may also process technical information necessary to ensure the security and correct operation of the website.

3. Contact form

Providing data is voluntary, but necessary for us to receive your inquiry and respond to it. Please do not include sensitive personal data or information that is not required to handle your inquiry in the message.

4. Purposes and legal basis

We process your data to respond to your inquiry and continue correspondence. The legal basis is our legitimate interest in communicating with website users and, where your inquiry is intended to conclude or perform a contract, taking steps at your request before entering into a contract or performing that contract.

5. Data retention period

We retain your data for the time needed to handle your inquiry and then for as long as is necessary to establish, pursue or defend possible claims, or until an effective objection is made where processing is based on our legitimate interest.

6. Data recipients

Data may be entrusted to service providers that support us in hosting, email and IT services, solely to the extent necessary to provide those services and under appropriate data protection arrangements.

7. Your rights

You have the right to request access to, rectification or erasure of your data, restriction of processing and data portability, and to object to processing. You may also lodge a complaint with the competent data protection authority.
